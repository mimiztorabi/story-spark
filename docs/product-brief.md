# Product Brief: Story Spark

## One-line concept

Story Spark helps caregivers turn a child’s interests into a short, reviewable story idea with safe creative boundaries.

## Problem

Caregivers may want a quick, personalized bedtime or quiet-time activity, but creating an original story can feel time-consuming. Generative AI can reduce the blank-page burden, yet families need predictable, age-appropriate output and clear adult control.

## Target audience

- **Primary:** caregivers of children ages 5–8 who want a five-minute shared storytelling activity.
- **Secondary:** educators exploring a guided creative-writing prompt.
- **Not a target:** unsupervised child use or open-ended social interaction.

## Product hypothesis

If caregivers can select a character, setting, and gentle lesson, then review a concise AI-assisted outline, they will feel more confident starting a personalized story activity.

## Goals

1. Make the first useful story idea available in under two minutes.
2. Make adult review and editing explicit before any story is read aloud.
3. Demonstrate a clear, safety-first AI product strategy in a portfolio format.

## Non-goals

- Replacing a caregiver’s judgment or creating a conversational companion.
- Storing family profiles, generating images, or publishing stories.
- Measuring real-world outcomes in this static demonstration.
